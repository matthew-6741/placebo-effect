Photo & asset credits

The food is fake. The photographers are real, and they deserve credit.

Nearly every photo in the app comes from Wikimedia Commons and is shared under a Creative Commons or public-domain license. Where a license asks for attribution, this page is that attribution. Photos are resized and cropped to fit their cards but are otherwise unchanged. Their authors don't endorse this app.
