The Placebo Effect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

Phantom is a novelty entertainment app. Orders, rides, trips, and payments in the app are simulated and never real. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for, accept, or store real credit/debit card numbers, CVCs, or bank details. The in-app "wallet" generates fake cards for display only.
  • We do not take any payments. Nothing is ever charged.
  • We do not require an account, name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your in-app progress (ghost count, simulated "spend," fake wallet cards, chosen city, squad list) in your browser/device local storage (localStorage). This stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing site data.

Images

Food photos are loaded from TheMealDB, and city/vehicle photos from LoremFlickr — both third-party image services. These are standard, anonymous image requests and contain no personal data.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app uses Google OAuth and the calendar.events permission solely to add playful "phantom" events to your primary calendar when you place a simulated order. The app does not read, store, or transmit your existing calendar data, and the connection is entirely optional and user-initiated. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Children

Phantom is a simple novelty app suitable for general audiences and collects no personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
