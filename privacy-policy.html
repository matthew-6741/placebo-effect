Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

The Placebo Effect is a novelty entertainment app. Orders, rides, trips, and payments in it are simulated and never real. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information, in plain language.

Who runs this

The Placebo Effect is an independent, non-commercial personal project — not a registered business, with no offices and no staff. There is no company behind it and nothing is sold. Questions, corrections, or takedown requests: matthewsanchez00024@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub. See also the Terms of Service.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for, accept, or store real credit or debit card numbers, CVCs, or bank details. The in-app "wallet" generates fake cards for display only.
  • We do not take payments. Nothing is ever charged.
  • We do not require an account. The optional sign-in is on-device only — your email is never transmitted, because there is no server to transmit it to.
  • We do not sell or share personal information. There is none to sell.

Analytics & consent

This site is set up to optionally use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people open. It is configured in assets/analytics.js and is switched off unless a Measurement ID has been added. While it's off — which it is today — no Google script loads, no analytics cookies are set, nothing is sent anywhere, and you won't see a cookie banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

If it is ever switched on, a consent banner appears first with equally weighted Accept and Reject buttons, and Google's script only loads if you choose Accept. Choosing Reject also clears any analytics cookies already set. Your choice is remembered on your device and can be changed any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, analytics never loads and you are never asked.

When enabled and accepted, Google receives standard web-analytics data: pages viewed, approximate location derived from a truncated (anonymized) IP address, device and browser type, and referring site. It is never linked to your in-app activity, your email, or your fake wallet.

Contact form

The form on the contact page does not submit anywhere. There is no inbox and no database behind it. What you type stays in your browser and is discarded when you navigate away or close the tab.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app uses Google OAuth and the calendar.events permission solely to add playful "phantom" events to your primary calendar when you place a simulated order. The app does not read, store, or transmit your existing calendar data, and the connection is entirely optional and user-initiated. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Use of AI

Parts of this website, its code, its copy, and its imagery were created with the help of AI tools. We think you should know that, so here is specifically where:

  • Code. Much of the app's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — including the ordering flow, maps, wallet, and these pages — was written with AI assistance and reviewed by a human before publishing.
  • Written copy. Menu descriptions, marketing emails, and page text were drafted with AI help and edited by a human.
  • The app icon and logo were generated using an AI image tool.
  • Food, city, and vehicle photography is not AI-generated — those are real photographs by real photographers, from Wikimedia Commons and TheMealDB, credited individually on the credits page.

AI was used as a building tool. It is not used to profile you, make decisions about you, or process any information you enter — nothing you type in this app is ever sent to an AI service, because nothing you type is sent anywhere at all.

Children & age

The app is a simple novelty suitable for general audiences, but it isn't directed at children under 13, and the Terms ask you to be 13 or older to use it. We collect no personal data from anyone, so there is nothing we could knowingly collect from a child either — what you type stays in your own browser.

The two features that involve a third party do have age rules: connecting Google Calendar requires a Google account (Google sets its own minimum age), and if analytics is ever enabled, the consent banner asks anyone under 16 to choose Reject. A parent or guardian can wipe everything the app has stored with the button below.

Delete your data

Because everything lives in your browser, you're the only one who can delete it — and you don't have to ask us. The button below erases every item the app has stored in this browser: ghost stats, fake wallet cards, squad, cuisine passport, sign-in, and settings. It can't be undone.

This only covers the browser you're using right now — repeat it on your other devices. You can also clear site data in your browser settings, which does the same thing. If you connected Google Calendar, the app drops its access when you delete, and you can also revoke it in your Google Account settings. Since we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no request for you to file.
